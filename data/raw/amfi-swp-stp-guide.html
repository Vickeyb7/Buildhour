AMFI Systematic Withdrawal & Transfer Plans

Source URL: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/swp.html

Type: core_concept

swp_mechanics

AMFI Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP): SWP allows unitholders to withdraw fixed amounts from their mutual fund at scheduled dates (e.g. monthly), providing regular cash flows while leaving remaining capital invested to grow.

stp_mechanics

AMFI Systematic Transfer Plan (STP): STP allows systematic transfer of a fixed sum from one scheme (e.g., a liquid fund) to another (e.g., an equity fund) within the same AMC, deploying lumpsum amounts gradually.
