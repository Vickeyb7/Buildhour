HDFC Request Statement & Capital Gains Portal

Source URL: https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/additional-info/request-statement

Type: statements

account_statement_request

HDFC Mutual Fund Statement Request: Unitholders can request an account statement or Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) via the official HDFC portal by entering their PAN, Folio number, or registered email ID with OTP validation.

capital_gains_statement

HDFC Mutual Fund Capital Gains Statement: Capital-gains statements for tax filing can be generated for any financial year (FY), separating Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) and Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) with grandfathering details.
