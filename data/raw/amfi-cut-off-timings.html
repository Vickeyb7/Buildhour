AMFI NAV Cut-Off Timings

Source URL: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/cut-off-timings.html

Type: operational

cut_off_rules_equity

AMFI Cut-off Timings for Equity/Debt Funds: Applications submitted and funds credited to the AMC bank account before 3:00 PM on a business day receive that day's closing NAV; orders after 3:00 PM receive next business day's NAV.

cut_off_rules_liquid

AMFI Cut-off Timings for Liquid and Overnight Funds: For subscriptions placed before 1:30 PM with funds realized, units are allotted at the previous day's NAV. For redemptions before 3:00 PM, same day NAV applies.
