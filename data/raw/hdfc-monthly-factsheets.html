HDFC Mutual Fund Monthly Factsheet: Published every month providing comprehensive portfolio disclosures, top 10 holdings, sector weightages, AUM, fund manager details, expense ratios, and historical trailing returns across all schemes.

portfolio_disclosure_frequency

HDFC Mutual Fund Portfolio Disclosure: In compliance with SEBI norms, monthly portfolio disclosures are uploaded on the 10th of every month, while fortnightly debt scheme portfolios are disclosed within 5 days of the fortnight ending.
