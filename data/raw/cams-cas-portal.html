CAMS Consolidated Account Statement Portal

Source URL: https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Consolidated-Account-Statement

Type: statements

cams_cas_generation

CAMS Consolidated Account Statement (CAS): Allows mutual fund investors to generate a single consolidated statement covering all mutual fund holdings and folios across all AMCs serviced by CAMS and KFintech using their registered email.

cams_capital_gains

CAMS Capital Gains & Realized Tax Statement: Provides detailed financial year capital gains statements categorized into short-term (STCG) and long-term (LTCG) with grandfathering dates for seamless income tax return (ITR) filing.
