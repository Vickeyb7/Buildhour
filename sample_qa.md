### Query 1: NAV Definition
**Question:** What is NAV and how is it calculated?
**Answer:** Net Asset Value (NAV) represents the per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme. It is calculated as: NAV = (Total Value of Assets − Liabilities and Expenses) ÷ Total Outstanding Units. Unlike stock prices which change continuously during market hours, mutual fund NAV is declared once each business day after the market closes at the end of trading.
**Citation Link:** https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/what-is-nav.html

### Query 2: Direct vs Regular Comparison
**Question:** What is the difference between Direct and Regular plans?
**Answer:** In a Direct Plan, you invest directly with the AMC with zero distributor commission, leading to a lower expense ratio and higher compounding returns over time. In a Regular Plan, you invest through an intermediary broker who receives ongoing commissions paid out of the fund's assets, resulting in a higher Total Expense Ratio (TER).
**Citation Link:** https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/direct-plan.html
