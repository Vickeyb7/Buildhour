SEBI Scheme Categorization & Rationalization

Source URL: https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2017/categorization-and-rationalization-of-mutual-fund-schemes_36199.html

Type: regulation

sebi_market_cap_definitions

SEBI Scheme Categorization Mandate: Defines Large Cap as 1st to 100th companies in full market capitalization, Mid Cap as 101st to 250th companies, and Small Cap as 251st company onwards, updated half-yearly by AMFI.

sebi_fund_categories

SEBI 5 Scheme Categories: Mutual fund schemes are classified into five categories: Equity Schemes (11 types), Debt Schemes (16 types), Hybrid Schemes (6 types), Solution Oriented Schemes (2 types), and Other Schemes (Index/ETFs and FoFs).
