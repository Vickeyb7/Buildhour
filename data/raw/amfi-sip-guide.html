sip_rupee_cost_averaging

AMFI SIP Rupee Cost Averaging: Systematic Investment Plans allow investors to invest fixed sums regularly, automatically buying more mutual fund units when market prices are low and fewer units when prices are high, smoothing purchase costs.

sip_compounding_benefits

AMFI SIP Compounding: Starting a regular SIP early harnesses compounding interest over long horizons, allowing reinvested earnings to generate additional returns without the need to time volatile equity markets.
