Type: charges

ter_caps_sebi

AMFI Expense Ratio Limits: SEBI mandates maximum TER caps based on AUM slabs: for equity schemes, up to 2.25% on the first ₹500 Cr, scaling down to 1.50% on ₹50,000 Cr+, ensuring economies of scale benefit retail investors.

ter_components

AMFI TER Components: Total Expense Ratio includes investment management fees, custodian fees, registrar and transfer agent fees, audit fees, marketing expenses, and statutory GST on management fees.
