nav_definition

AMFI – What is NAV: Net Asset Value (NAV) is the market value of all securities held by a mutual fund scheme minus liabilities, divided by total outstanding units. It represents the price per unit at which investors buy or redeem fund units.

nav_calculation_frequency

AMFI NAV Calculation: Mutual fund NAV is calculated daily at the close of trading hours and must be uploaded to the AMFI portal by 11:00 PM on every business day. NAV changes daily based on underlying market price movements.
