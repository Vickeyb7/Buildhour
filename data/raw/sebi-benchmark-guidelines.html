SEBI Guiding Principles for Scheme Benchmarks

Type: regulation

tier_benchmarks_framework

SEBI Benchmarking Framework: Introduces a two-tier benchmark system: Tier 1 benchmark reflects the broad category mandate (e.g. Nifty 50 TRI for Large Cap), while Tier 2 benchmark reflects the specific fund investment style or strategy.
