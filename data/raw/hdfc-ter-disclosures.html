HDFC Total Expense Ratio Disclosures

Source URL: https://www.hdfcfund.com/statutory-disclosure/total-expense-ratio-ter

Type: charges

daily_ter_disclosure

HDFC Mutual Fund Daily Total Expense Ratio (TER): All mutual funds are mandated by SEBI to disclose the base TER and total TER of both Regular and Direct plans on their website daily, reflecting management fees, operational costs, and taxes.

direct_plan_ter_discount

HDFC Mutual Fund TER Differential: Direct plans have a lower expense ratio compared to Regular plans because no distributor commissions, marketing commissions, or agent trail fees are charged to the scheme in Direct plans.
