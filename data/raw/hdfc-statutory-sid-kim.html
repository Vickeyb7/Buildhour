HDFC Scheme Information Document & KIM

Source URL: https://www.hdfcfund.com/statutory-disclosure/scheme-information-document-sid

Type: statutory

sid_purpose

HDFC Mutual Fund – Scheme Information Document (SID): The legal statutory document setting forth the scheme's investment objective, asset allocation, investment strategies, risk factors, fee structure, and unitholder rights as approved by SEBI.

kim_purpose

HDFC Mutual Fund – Key Information Memorandum (KIM): An abbreviated summary of the Scheme Information Document containing critical information an investor must know before making an investment decision, attached to every application form.
